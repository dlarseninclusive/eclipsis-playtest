THE PLAYTEST PROCEDURE

Eclipsis · The Shattered Expanse

Thirty minutes of your time, spent well

‹ Back to downloads

1 · Get the build running

No installer, no launcher, no account. Download from the front page, unzip anywhere, and run eclipsis.exe (Windows) or ./eclipsis.x86_64 (Linux). If the game offers to set up the AI narrator, that is optional — everything works without it.

  • Core keys: WASD move · E use / enter · Space attack · B build mode · I backpack · Esc menu & settings (audio sliders live there). The game shows the rest as you meet it.
  • Your saves live at %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Eclipsis — Godot Spike\ on Windows, ~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/Eclipsis — Godot Spike/ on Linux. Saves from older playtests load in the current build — that is itself worth testing.

2 · What this build needs eyes on

Focus for Playtest 10

  • Spend an evening at the bar. Sit on a stool — the keeper should greet you and serve you right there. Order a drink from the seat, close the screen and stay seated, stand up. Then check the shelf: about half the bars sell water and food to go; a bare house should say so. Call out any bar where the seat is dead or the shelf lies.
  • Buy an ox and abuse it. Buy it from inside a building — it should be waiting at the door, not standing in the tap room. Walk, shop, take a tunnel: it should follow, wait outside, and find you on the far side. Load it up close (it should refuse at a distance) and watch the panniers swell — and the tag count up, “3/40”, not “100%”.
  • Drop things. Backpack tab: arrows pick, - / = set the count, D drops. The pile should sit at your feet without bouncing back into your pack, and E should take it back. Fair warning: piles don’t survive a save yet, and scavengers may rob an unattended stash.
  • Follow somebody important. Tail a faction head on their rounds. They should walk around buildings — never through one — wearing gear worth their title, weapon in hand. Bring one down and their kit should be on the ground.
  • Watch the clock, then race it. The TIME pill sits beside the clock at every speed — click it or press .. Does anything about fast time feel wrong? And the yellow ring: it should only ever appear when you’re steering a companion, labeled.
  • Lean on the console. Tab completes commands and names — tp rust + Tab — and every answer now prints in the console’s own pane, no message log needed. An answer that lies, a completion that misfires, or a name that won’t paste back is a bug.

3 · The console is your test rig

Press ` (backquote, left of the 1 key) to open the in-game console. These are testing tools, not the intended way to play — but they get you to the interesting places fast.

  • help — lists all seventeen commands with their usage; help <command> spells one out.
  • god / heal — survive your own experiments; heal also clears injuries and bleeding.
  • give <item> [n] and coronas <n> — goods into your pack, coin into your purse.
  • tp <x> <y> or tp <town name> — jump anywhere, or to any settlement by name.
  • spawn [type] [faction] — put a real NPC on the ground beside you to poke at.
  • time <hour> — set the clock (decimal hours: 23.5 is half past eleven at night).
  • storm / eclipse — summon the weather and the dying sun instead of waiting.
  • scenario [id] — apply a whole different start to the running game; bare lists them.

4 · Report what you find

  • One-time setup (~2 minutes): make a free GitHub account at github.com/signup — email, password, username, done. Then on the project’s GitHub page click Watch → Custom → Releases so every new build lands in your email.
  • File a bug at the issue tracker — one issue per thing, and the form will walk you through it. Drag screenshots straight into the text box. You get emailed automatically when your bug is answered or a fix ships. Include which playtest build (it is on the title screen), your OS, what happened, and what you expected instead. Screenshots help enormously; a save file that shows the problem helps even more.
  • No GitHub account? Send it to Dave directly, same details.
  • Nothing is too small. A word that reads oddly, a sound that is too loud, a door that felt wrong — all of it is signal.

5 · Hear about new builds

  • Email on every release: on the GitHub repo, click Watch → Custom → Releases. GitHub emails you each time a new playtest is published. (Free account needed.)
  • No account: point any RSS reader or RSS-to-email service at the releases feed.
  • What changed in each build is always on the release notes page.
