GX-C — the plate is real, the hood is not reachable

The talk card carries a 112×126 portrait: a drawn face, hair, skin, the speaker's name beneath it. It reads at play size, which is the thing that was in doubt.

The hooded path could not be exercised in play, as predicted before the cut. road_hood is on no shelf, no loot row and no loadout, so the only hooded body a generated world holds is a faction leader in a crowned helm. I reached a leader's parley screen but not a leader's card inside the gate's time budget; a console give proves the item exists, not the consumer path. Not claimed as tested. WD1 puts hoods on scouts, the Choir, the Penumbra and the low bandit tiers; the 14f12 drive meets one on a road, which is the honest test.

WR1 — 171 lines, verified on the table, not in the mouth

check_fallbacks.py passes the shipped table: 19 factions, 171 lines, 63 carrying a slot (37%), no unknown token, every faction exercising {ADDRESS}, pools 2–4 ASCII terms. What I could not do is photograph one being spoken. Forcing the canned path (model no-such-model) and talking to gate guards produced a frisk card and a parley menu rather than a faction line; the bodies that answer with a fallback line were not in reach in the runs I had time for. The lines are right in the data and the resolver is unit-checked upstream; the sentence a player hears remains unphotographed.

Cinematic mode — one nuance worth writing down

Launch flag, F10 and the console all hide and restore the HUD as specified. A save-and-reload inside the same session keeps the HUD hidden — my drive's after_reload frame reads HUD-off. That is not a defect: the state is a view preference, not a save field, and a fresh launch without the flag shows the HUD (every other part of this gate proves it). Worth stating so nobody later reads the frame as a bug.
