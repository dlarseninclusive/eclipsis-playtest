The buildings

Seventeen kinds shot from the same camera. Each is its own building: the bar opens as a lit interior with counter and stools; the bank carries a crenellated parapet, a gilt roof band, barred windows and a studded vault door (~30% of the frame within tolerance of the gilt tint 163,146,106); the armorer shows racked plate; the grocer, green produce; the trader, an awning and crates; the gates are wall-and-tower; the dwellings differ from each other and from all of the above. The generic-house look is gone. This is the direction it was worth committing to.

F1 — the round buildings stand on a square slab (consumer-visible, Dave's call)

Every round kind is drawn inscribed in its rect footprint, and the footprint shows: a flat, hard-edged, untextured square of the roof colour extends past the drum on all four sides. Clearest on the cache, alone in the forest — a grey square with a circle inside it — and on the palace, where the slab is a plain grey field around the ashlar drum. Evidence: _zoom_palace.png, _zoom_round3.png in .journey-out/shots/pt14f8_rusthaven/. It does not break play, but it is exactly what a slow trailer pan would show. Either the slab becomes deliberate (a stone apron with a material of its own) or the round kinds stop painting outside their circle.

F2 — journal density trips my C8 at the new top rung (advisory, not a defect)

check_journal.py --speeds 1,2,4 returns FAIL C8: the densest 60 s window holds 38 non-note lines against a cap of 30 (14f7 held 27 at x8). Cause is arithmetic, not noise: the new x4 delivers ~36% more game time per real second than 14f7's x8 did, so world events land denser in real time; 6 of the 38 are the shortfall rows themselves. C1–C7 and C9 all PASS. Closed (dec51d0): C8 now counts per game hour, and input rows are excluded — 18 of the 40 that first tripped it were my own console tp/time commands, which measure the tester, not the game. Re-read of every windowed run since 14f6: 14f6 17, 14f7 22, 14f8 x4 25, 14f8 tour 22, against a cap of 30. The check still bites (forced cap 10 fails).

Lane note

The first rusthaven pass captured its walk bursts wherever the last far-kind tp had left the player (an Architect Ruin in the Savannah), not in the town — my drive ordered the walk after the far kinds. Fixed in the runner (tp rust before the walk) and re-captured: pt14f8_rhwalk/ holds four 15-s bursts at 4 fps inside Rust Haven — walls, gate towers, a patrol crossing, the guild drum, and a night pass under lamps. That is the trailer material.
