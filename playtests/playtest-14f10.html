The x1/x2 window also moved, 25.92 → 27.30 ms. That is a real cost, in line with the dev box's +2.6% draw_world / +6.3% lid-off bar, and it is the first slab in this run of cuts to spend frames. It buys dressing the player looks straight at, so it is worth it once; it should not be spent twice. Nothing here is near the cliff — the top rung still delivers more world per minute than 14f9 did.

What reads

The bar is a bar: counter, kegs and bottles on the shelf, stools, a lit brazier, the keeper behind it with [F] trade. The smithy is a smithy: workbench with tools laid out, brick hearth, rack, and a grindstone in the next room. Outside, the trader's crates read as crates at play zoom — lid boards and a stencil on the face, not tan boxes — the grocer has produce and a stalled awning, and the cache's yard has crates in the corner. A crate reads as a crate, which was the claim.

Not reached, and why

  • The cell. No generated world contains a gaol or a slaver camp — the compound is raised around

you when you are taken — so a teleport census cannot reach one. Testing it needs a custody run (get taken, then look), which is its own drive; it is not in this gate and I have not claimed it. The orchestrator's inherited bug (a gaol raised by send_to_gaol renders no interior dressing at all) is therefore also unconfirmed from my side, and is named on the release page as inherited.

  • The grocer's and trader's interiors. The venue doors sit at different distances from the tp

point, so a fixed walk-in enters some and walks past others. The runner now shoots each venue twice (2.2 s and 3.6 s of walk, E at each) and the read takes whichever frame is inside; those two were outside in both. Their street dressing is what is measured here instead.

Not tested

Interiors above the ground floor; gates as gates; macOS and Linux artifacts; long-run stability; whether the interior dressing survives the eclipse's light change.

Follow-on: what the 14f11 drive adds

  • faces (GX-C). Talk cards from the gate mouth at Rust Haven — two shown-face speakers, then

give road_hood as the mechanism proof. Stated plainly in the verdict: with road_hood on no shelf, no loot row and no loadout, the only hooded body a generated world contains is a faction leader in a crowned helm, so the hooded card in 14f11 is NOT the consumer path. WD1 puts hoods on scouts, the Choir, the Penumbra and the low bandit tiers for 14f12; the 14f12 drive meets one on the road.

  • cinematic (ST5-B). Every capture part re-shot with --cinematic once it exists: the acceptance

is zero HUD rows in the art log and no hotbar plate pixels in the bottom band, and it is what makes the trailer beats worth recording.

  • store frames 08–11 (ST5). The four new page shots re-taken from the kit at 1080p, and the seven

existing ones unchanged by md5 — the determinism fix is what makes that assertion possible.
