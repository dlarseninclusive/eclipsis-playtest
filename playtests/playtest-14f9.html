F1 is closed — the round buildings have a yard

The flat grey square is gone. Every round kind now sits on an octagonal apron: chamfered corners, a kerb line, flagstone joints, and one or two crates in the corner the door arc cannot reach. On the cache alone in the forest — the acceptance frame, tp 36604 92155 — it reads as a paved yard around a well-head rather than a box with a circle in it. Measured tones: apron 121,111,93 against forest ground 153,150,120, with the drum's shadow at 131,126,102 falling on ground beyond the kerb.

F4 (minor, visual) — at 1:1 the apron reads as a tone plate, not as paving

The flagstone texture is real in the data and visible at 4x, but at the zoom the game is played at the courses and joints fall below the eye: what reads is a darker octagon with an outline. Stronger joint contrast, or a course or two of edge-aligned slabs, would earn the detail at the size it is seen. Not blocking; the box-with-a-circle problem it was raised against is fixed.

Carried from the landing, not defects: the shadow starts outside the kerb on palace and guild by arithmetic (rise 3.2), so the shadow-on-yard clause is met on cache and kiln only; round yards read paler than rect court/hall yards. Both were declared before the cut.

GX-B — there are two body plans in the crowd, and they read

Rust Haven's own streets are thin at the tp point, so the crowd read is taken from the deterministic town frame at this head (store_shots_0224b82_run1/01_town_day.png). That camera is wider than play zoom — bodies ~18 px tall against ~30 px in the window — so it is the harder test, not the easier one.

Two clusters with a gap, and the crops confirm it by eye: _crowd_bodies_0224b82.png shows square straight-sided trunks beside trunks that narrow at the waist and widen at the hip, in the same gear. At the window's own zoom the three gate guards separate the same way (pt14f9_crowd/_gate_bodies.png).

Qualified how: at this size the signal is one to two pixels and it is carried mostly by the hip line, so a wide sash, a held weapon or a bad facing can mask it on a given body. It is readable in a crowd, not readable on every body every frame. The 1.17 rows are pixel quantisation (6 px → 7 px is a 1.167 step), not a caricature bound breach — gxb1's ≤ 1.15 is measured in the table, not in pixels.
