# Eclipsis: The Shattered Expanse

*Survive a wasteland that keeps living whether you are in it or not.*

**Eclipsis: The Shattered Expanse** is an open-world survival sim in the Kenshi
lane. Nineteen factions hold their own cities, feed their own people and go to
war without asking you. You start as nobody in particular, and the world keeps
a record of what you did to it.

This repository hosts the **public playtest builds** for Windows and Linux.

## Play it

- **[Download the current playtest](https://dlarseninclusive.github.io/eclipsis-playtest/)** — single executable, no installer, no launcher, no account.
- **[The playtest procedure](https://dlarseninclusive.github.io/eclipsis-playtest/playtest.html)** — what to try, the in-game console, and how to report what you find.
- **[Release notes](https://dlarseninclusive.github.io/eclipsis-playtest/notes.html)** — what changed in every build, newest first.

## Adding a devlog post

The devlog is one file, `devlog.html`, a dated feed with the newest entry first. Each entry is one
`<section class="rel">` block. To add a daily post, copy the existing `Daily post` block, put it above the
newest entry, and change the date (in the `rel-date` line and the two `p2026-..` ids), the title, the
one-line summary and the bullet list. A video rollup is the same block with `Video rollup` in the date line,
plus a page at `devlog/N.html` (copy `devlog/1.html`) and its YouTube link. Link new posts only to things that
have shipped; mark anything not shipped as coming, in its own post when it ships.

## Found a bug?

[File it on the issue tracker](https://github.com/dlarseninclusive/eclipsis-playtest/issues) —
the form walks you through it. Include which playtest build (it is on the title
screen), your OS, what happened, and what you expected instead.

## Hear about new builds

Click **Watch → Custom → Releases** on this repository and GitHub will email
you every time a new playtest is published. No account? Point any RSS reader at
the [releases feed](https://github.com/dlarseninclusive/eclipsis-playtest/releases.atom).
